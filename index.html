I’m a founder at Unravel. We help clients build agentic systems—software that can take on tasks, use tools, and get things done.

Alongside client work, we’re building products of our own. That means working on both the software itself and the decisions around it: what to build, how it should work, and how people will use it.

My role moves between engineering, product, and design, with a hand in the everyday work of running the company.

02 — A BIT OF BACKGROUND

Code. Products.
People.

I started as a developer and went on to lead engineering teams as a Director of Engineering. Along the way, my work expanded into product and design, and into helping teams grow.

Some of that work was technical. Some was helping engineers make the move into management: figuring out how to lead people, have difficult conversations, and find their own way as a manager.

Today, being a founder brings those different parts of my experience together.
